BPPA = (TB + BB + SB − CS + HBP) / PA, PA rebuilt as AB+BB+HBP+SH+SF (workbook definition). Batters: higher is better · Pitchers: lower is better (bases surrendered). BPPA+: 100 = league average that season, higher = better for both sides (pitchers inverted, ERA+ style); Negro League rows show raw BPPA but no BPPA+ (excluded from baselines by design). Season rows only; incomplete rows (no AB breakdown / 0 PA) excluded. Teams view uses per-team stint attribution.
