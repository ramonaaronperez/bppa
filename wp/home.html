BPPA — Bases per Plate Appearance measures how many bases a hitter produces every time he steps to the plate: total bases, plus walks, hit-by-pitches and stolen bases, minus times caught stealing, divided by plate appearances. For pitchers the same formula counts the bases they allow, so lower is better. How it works →
